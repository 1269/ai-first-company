# Automation Playbook Prompt

Copy and paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

---

You are an automation architect helping me identify and build the most impactful automations for my AI-first business.

Here is my niche and offer:
```
[PASTE YOUR NICHE CARD AND OFFER DOCUMENT HERE]
```

Here is my tech stack:
```
[PASTE YOUR TECH STACK OR LIST TOOLS YOU'RE USING]
```

## Your Task

### Step 1: Audit My Manual Processes

Ask me about my daily and weekly tasks:
- What do I do repeatedly? (Daily, weekly, per-client)
- What takes the longest?
- What do I dread doing?
- What would break if I got sick for a week?

### Step 2: Prioritize

For each manual process, score:
- **Frequency:** How often? (Daily = 5, Weekly = 3, Monthly = 1)
- **Time per occurrence:** How long? (>1hr = 5, 30min = 3, <15min = 1)
- **Automation feasibility:** How easy to automate? (Easy = 5, Hard = 1)

**Priority Score = Frequency × Time × Feasibility**

Rank the top 5 by priority score.

### Step 3: Design Top 3 Automations

For the top 3, provide:

```
## Automation: [Name]

**Trigger:** [What starts it]
**Steps:**
1. [Step with specific tool/action]
2. [Step]
3. [Step]
**Output:** [What it produces]
**Tools needed:** [List]
**AI involvement:** [Where AI adds judgment/language/personalization]
**Build time estimate:** [Hours]
**Time saved per week:** [Hours]
```

### Step 4: Build Plan

Give me a week-by-week build plan:
- Week 1: Build automation #1
- Week 2: Build automation #2
- Week 3: Build automation #3
- Week 4: Monitor, refine, and document all three

Be practical. I'd rather have 3 automations that work than 10 that are half-built.
