# The 12-Week Year

A sprint-based execution framework. Instead of planning in annual cycles (where most people quit by March), plan in 12-week sprints where every week matters.

## Why 12 Weeks?

- **Urgency.** 12 weeks is short enough that you can't waste 3 weeks and "catch up later."
- **Focus.** You can only pursue 1-2 major initiatives in 12 weeks. Forces prioritization.
- **Feedback loops.** You learn what works every 12 weeks, not every 12 months.
- **Fresh starts.** Didn't hit your goal? Reset in 12 weeks, not 12 months.

## The Structure

### Quarterly Quests

Each 12-week sprint has:
- **1 Main Quest** — The primary goal. If you hit nothing else, hit this.
- **2-3 Side Quests** — Supporting goals that complement the Main Quest.

Split into two domains:
- **Work Quests** — Business and career goals.
- **Life Quests** — Health, family, personal growth.

### Weekly Planning

Every week:
1. **Review last week** — Did I execute the plan? What worked? What didn't?
2. **Score the week** — Grade yourself honestly (0-100% on planned actions completed).
3. **Plan next week** — What are the 3-5 most important actions?

### The Weekly Review (30-60 Minutes)

Do this the same time every week. Sunday morning works well.

```
## Weekly Review: Week [X] of 12

### Execution Score: [X]%
(Planned actions completed / planned actions total)

### Wins
- [What went well]

### Misses
- [What didn't happen and why]

### Key Metrics
- [Metric 1]: [Value]
- [Metric 2]: [Value]

### Next Week: Top 3 Actions
1. [Most important thing]
2. [Second most important]
3. [Third most important]

### Adjustments
[Any changes to the plan based on what I learned]
```

## 12-Week Year + AI-First Company

### Sprint 1 (Weeks 1-12): Discovery & First Revenue

**Work Main Quest:** Close first paying client.
**Work Side Quests:** Define niche, build offer, launch website.
**Life Main Quest:** Establish morning routine (4 AM wake, deep work by 5).

### Sprint 2 (Weeks 13-24): Scale & Systematize

**Work Main Quest:** Hit $X/month recurring revenue.
**Work Side Quests:** Build content engine, create lead gen system, hire first AI agent.
**Life Main Quest:** Consistent fitness routine (3x/week).

### Sprint 3 (Weeks 25-36): Leverage & Grow

**Work Main Quest:** Productize the offer (move from DFY to DWY).
**Work Side Quests:** Launch community, build agent library, create case studies.
**Life Main Quest:** [Your life goal here].

### Sprint 4 (Weeks 37-48): Optimize & Expand

**Work Main Quest:** [Based on what you learned in sprints 1-3].
**Work Side Quests:** [Based on what you learned].
**Life Main Quest:** [Your life goal here].

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too many quests.** Stick to 1 main + 2-3 side per domain. If everything is a priority, nothing is.
2. **Not scoring weekly.** If you don't track execution, you'll drift. Score honestly.
3. **Moving the goal.** If you realize the goal was wrong, note it in your review and pivot — but don't just silently lower the bar.
4. **Skipping the review.** The review IS the system. Miss it and the whole thing falls apart.
