# Skills Audit

**Phase 0: Discover → Step 1**

Before you can build an AI-first company, you need to know what you bring to the table. This isn't a résumé exercise — it's about identifying the intersection of what you know, what you've done, and where AI can multiply your impact.

## Why This Matters

Most people skip this step and jump straight to "I'll build an AI app." Then they build something nobody wants because it wasn't grounded in real expertise or real problems.

Your unfair advantage isn't AI itself — everyone has access to AI. Your unfair advantage is **domain knowledge + AI leverage**.

## The Audit

Work through these three layers. Be specific. "I know marketing" is useless. "I've run paid acquisition for B2B SaaS companies spending $50K-500K/month" is gold.

### Layer 1: Professional Skills

What have you been paid to do?

- What's your current role? What do you actually do day-to-day?
- What roles have you held in the last 10 years?
- What tools, platforms, and technologies do you use professionally?
- What problems do people come to you to solve?
- What have you built that people actually use?

### Layer 2: Domain Knowledge

What industries and verticals do you understand deeply?

- What industries have you worked in?
- What business models do you understand from the inside?
- What jargon do you speak fluently that outsiders wouldn't understand?
- What pain points have you seen repeatedly that nobody has fixed well?
- What processes have you watched people do manually that made you think "this could be automated"?

### Layer 3: Unique Combinations

The magic is usually at the intersection. What combinations make you unusual?

- What's a weird combination of skills you have that most people don't?
- What do you know about that you didn't learn in school or at work?
- What side projects, hobbies, or obsessions give you insight others lack?
- If you had to teach a 2-hour workshop tomorrow, what would it be on?

## Running the Prompt

Use the prompt in `prompts/skills-audit-prompt.md` with your AI assistant to run an interactive skills audit session. It will ask you questions, help you dig deeper, and produce a structured output you can use in the next steps.

```bash
# Claude Code
/audit-skills

# Or copy-paste the prompt from:
cat prompts/skills-audit-prompt.md
```

## Output

After completing the audit, you should have:

1. **Skills inventory** — A structured list of your professional skills, domain knowledge, and unique combinations.
2. **Top 3-5 domains** — The industries or verticals where you have the deepest expertise.
3. **Automation candidates** — Processes you've seen that are manual, repetitive, and painful.

Save your output. You'll use it in the next step: **[Market Fit](market-fit.md)**.
