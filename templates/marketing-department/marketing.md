## Boundary

- **Marketing owns how growth works:** pages, campaigns, email, social and launches.
- **The brand keeps its identity.** Voice, design and colours live in [WHERE YOUR BRAND LIVES]. Marketing uses them and never copies them.
- **Facts have one home.** Prices and offer details live where the table above says. Read them there.
- **Sales takes over at the conversation.** Once someone is talking to the owner one to one, it is sales, not marketing.

## Who does what

Starting picks, not a ranking. Change them for any job in that job's task document.

| Step | Who | Writes to |
|------|-----|-----------|
| Research | [THE RESEARCHER, FOR EXAMPLE GEMINI IN ANTIGRAVITY] | Research |
| Plan | every tool answers the same question; the owner picks one answer | Plan |
| Build | [THE BUILDER, FOR EXAMPLE CODEX] | Build notes |
| Review | a different model from the builder | Review |

- **Pick the answer, not the brand.** The planning question goes to every tool, answered in the chat, and only the plan the owner picks is written down.
- **The reviewer is never the builder.** A model is a little more critical of another model's work than of its own. Tell the reviewer to argue: what is wrong or weak, worst first. With only one tool, the review runs in a fresh session that is told to argue with the work.
- **The driver is whichever tool the owner is typing in.** It can hand work to the others, or the owner can carry it between them.

## Gated actions

An agent that reaches one of these stops and asks the owner in the session, and waits for a yes.

- **Spend:** any paid ad, paid tool or plan, or paid data.
- **Publish:** anything that goes public: a post, a page or site change, a campaign going live, a push that deploys a website.
- **Send:** any email or newsletter to a list, and any message to a person outside the business.
- **Sign:** any agreement, partnership, sponsorship, or terms acceptance.

## Review cadence

Monthly: which jobs worked, whether the picks in Who does what still fit, and what to drop.
