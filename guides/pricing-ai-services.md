# Pricing AI Services

How to price your AI consulting, automation, and agent-building services. The short version: charge for the outcome, not the hours.

## The Value Pricing Mindset

**Hourly pricing punishes efficiency.** If you build an AI agent in 4 hours that saves a client $200K/year, charging $150/hour gets you $600. That's insane.

**Value pricing rewards outcomes.** That same agent, priced at 5-10% of first-year value, is a $10K-20K project. The client still gets a 10x return. You get paid fairly.

## The Hormozi Value Equation

```
            Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
            Time to Result × Effort Required
```

Your price should reflect the value, not the cost. AI dramatically improves all four variables:
- **Dream Outcome** — AI can deliver outcomes that weren't previously possible.
- **Perceived Likelihood** — A working demo or proof-of-concept increases trust.
- **Time to Result** — AI solutions can be deployed in days, not months.
- **Effort Required** — Done-for-you with AI means near-zero effort for the client.

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Done-For-You Projects — $5,000 to $50,000+

You build the entire solution. Best for:
- AI agents and automations
- Custom integrations
- Data pipelines and analytics
- Process automation

**How to price:**
1. Estimate the annual value of the outcome (time saved × hourly cost, or revenue generated).
2. Price at 5-15% of Year 1 value.
3. Minimum project fee: $5,000. Below this, the overhead isn't worth it.

**Example:**
- Client's sales team spends 20 hours/week on lead research.
- 20 hours × $75/hour × 52 weeks = $78,000/year in labor cost.
- Your AI agent handles 80% of this = $62,400/year in savings.
- Price: $10,000-15,000 for the build + $1,000/month maintenance.

### Tier 2: Done-With-You Engagements — $1,000 to $10,000

You guide the client through implementation with templates, prompts, and support. Best for:
- AI strategy workshops
- Implementation sprints (you guide, they build)
- Training teams to use AI tools effectively

**How to price:**
- Half-day workshop: $2,000-5,000
- 4-week implementation sprint: $5,000-10,000
- Monthly advisory: $1,000-3,000/month

### Tier 3: Products & Templates — $50 to $500

Productized solutions the client uses independently. Best for:
- Agent templates and configurations
- Automation workflow templates
- Prompt libraries for specific use cases
- Self-serve tools

**How to price:**
- One-time purchase: $50-500
- Monthly subscription: $29-199/month
- These require significant volume to generate meaningful revenue

## The Pricing Conversation

Never lead with price. Follow this sequence:

1. **Understand the pain** — "What's this costing you today?" (Time, money, missed opportunities.)
2. **Quantify the outcome** — "If we solve this, what's that worth over the next 12 months?"
3. **Present the investment** — "Based on the $X outcome, the investment for this project is $Y."
4. **Frame the ROI** — "That's a Z-to-1 return in the first year alone."

If the client focuses on hours, redirect: "I don't charge by the hour because that would penalize me for being efficient. I charge based on the value of the outcome."

## Common Pricing Mistakes

1. **Charging hourly.** You're an AI-first builder. Your speed is the feature, not a discount.
2. **Undercharging to "get experience."** Your first client sets the anchor. Start at $5K minimum.
3. **Not including maintenance.** AI solutions need monitoring and updates. Charge $500-2,000/month.
4. **Scope creep without re-pricing.** Define what's included. Everything else is a change order.
5. **Discounting before being asked.** Never negotiate against yourself.

## Guarantee Options

Reduce buyer risk without giving away the farm:

- **Performance guarantee** — "If the agent doesn't achieve X metric in 30 days, I'll keep working until it does at no extra cost."
- **Satisfaction guarantee** — "If you're not satisfied after the first milestone, I'll refund the remaining project fee."
- **Pilot pricing** — "Let's start with a $2,500 pilot. If results are strong, we continue with the full project."

Avoid unconditional money-back guarantees on Done-For-You work. Your time has real cost.
