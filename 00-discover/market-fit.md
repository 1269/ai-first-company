# Market Fit

**Phase 0: Discover → Step 2**

You've audited your skills. Now the question: **where does what you know meet what people will pay for?**

This step is about finding the overlap between your expertise and real market pain — specifically pain that AI can solve better, faster, or cheaper than current solutions.

## The Market Fit Framework

Great AI-first businesses sit at the intersection of three circles:

```
        Your Skills
            ∩
      Market Pain  →  AI-First Business
            ∩
      AI Leverage
```

- **Your Skills** — What you know deeply (from the Skills Audit).
- **Market Pain** — Problems people are actively spending money or time trying to solve.
- **AI Leverage** — Where AI creates a 10x improvement, not a 10% improvement.

If you only have two of three, you get:
- Skills + Pain (no AI leverage) = A consulting business, not an AI company.
- Skills + AI (no market pain) = A cool demo nobody pays for.
- Pain + AI (no skills) = A business someone else is better positioned to build.

## Finding Market Pain

For each of your top domains from the Skills Audit, answer:

### Who's in pain?
- What specific role or persona deals with this problem daily?
- Are they a decision-maker who can buy, or do they need approval?
- How many of these people exist? (Hundreds? Thousands? Millions?)

### What's the pain?
- What task takes them way too long?
- What process is embarrassingly manual?
- Where are they losing money, leads, or time?
- What do they complain about in forums, Slack communities, or on calls?

### How are they solving it today?
- What tools or services do they currently use?
- How much are they spending?
- What's broken about the current solution?

### Where does AI create 10x leverage?
- Can AI do this task in seconds instead of hours?
- Can AI do it at a quality level that wasn't previously possible?
- Can AI enable a solo operator to do what previously required a team?
- Is there a data advantage (the more you use it, the better it gets)?

## Output

After this step, you should have:

1. **2-3 validated pain points** — Specific problems in specific markets with evidence of demand.
2. **AI leverage thesis** — For each pain point, a clear statement of how AI makes the solution dramatically better.
3. **Competitive landscape snapshot** — What exists today and why your approach is different.
