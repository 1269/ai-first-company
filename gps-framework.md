# GPS Framework: Goal → Plan → System

A decision-making and execution framework for AI-first builders.

## The Problem

Most builders do one of two things:
1. Set goals but never plan how to achieve them.
2. Build systems but lose sight of why they're building them.

GPS forces alignment between the destination (Goal), the route (Plan), and the vehicle (System).

## The Framework

### Goal — Where Are You Going?

A good goal is:
- **Specific** — Not "grow the business" but "reach $10K MRR."
- **Time-bound** — "By the end of this 12-week sprint."
- **Measurable** — You can look at a number and know if you hit it.
- **Meaningful** — It moves the needle, not just the metric.

Write it as a single sentence:

> "By [date], I will [specific outcome] as measured by [metric]."

### Plan — What's the Route?

The plan breaks the goal into milestones and actions:

1. **Milestones** — 3-4 checkpoints between here and the goal.
2. **Weekly actions** — What you do each week to hit the next milestone.
3. **Lead measures** — Activities you control (calls made, content published, code shipped) vs. lag measures (revenue, signups) that you can't control directly.

The plan answers: **What do I do this week?**

### System — What's the Vehicle?

The system is the repeatable process that makes execution automatic:

- **Time blocks** — When do you do the work? (Same time, every day/week.)
- **Tools** — What do you use? (Set up once, use forever.)
- **Automations** — What happens without you? (Workflows, agents, scheduled tasks.)
- **Reviews** — When do you check progress? (Weekly review, non-negotiable.)

The system answers: **How do I make sure it happens consistently?**

## GPS in Practice

### Example: Launch an AI Consulting Offer

**Goal:** By March 31, generate $15K in consulting revenue from 3 Done-For-You AI automation projects.

**Plan:**
- Week 1-2: Define offer and ICP. Build one-pager.
- Week 3-4: Outreach to 50 prospects. Book 10 discovery calls.
- Week 5-8: Close 3 projects. Begin delivery on first project.
- Week 9-12: Deliver all 3 projects. Collect testimonials. Refine offer for next quarter.

**System:**
- Tuesday + Wednesday mornings: Outbound outreach (30 min each).
- Thursday afternoon: Follow-up on all open conversations.
- Lead research agent runs on new prospects automatically.
- Weekly review every Sunday: pipeline status, revenue progress, next week's priorities.
