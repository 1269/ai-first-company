# Offer Design Prompt

Copy and paste this entire prompt into your AI assistant. Replace the placeholder with your niche card.

---

You are a pricing and offer strategist. Help me design my first offer for my AI-first company.

Here is my niche:
```
[PASTE YOUR NICHE CARD / MY_NICHE.md HERE]
```

## Your Task

### Step 1: Understand the Value

Ask me 5-7 questions about my ideal customer's current situation:
- How much time do they spend on this problem weekly?
- What does that time cost them? (Salary, opportunity cost)
- What's the revenue impact of solving this? (More leads, faster closes, fewer errors)
- Have they tried other solutions? What did they pay?
- How quickly do they need results?

### Step 2: Design the Offer

Based on my answers, design a Done-For-You (Tier 1) offer:

```
## Offer: [Name]

**One-liner:** [What the client gets in one sentence]

**The Problem:**
[What the client is dealing with — be specific and painful]

**The Solution:**
[What you build/deliver — be concrete]

**What's Included:**
- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]
- [Support/maintenance terms]

**What's NOT Included:**
- [Boundary 1]
- [Boundary 2]

**Timeline:** [X weeks from kickoff to delivery]

**Investment:** $[Price]

**Why This Price:**
[Value-based justification — ROI calculation showing it's a no-brainer]

**Guarantee:**
[Risk reversal — what happens if they're not satisfied?]
```

### Step 3: Stress Test

Challenge the offer:
- Is the price too low? (Most first-time founders undercharge.)
- Is the scope too broad? (Can I actually deliver this in the timeline?)
- Is the guarantee too risky? (Could I get burned?)
- Would I buy this if I were the customer?

### Step 4: Create the One-Pager

Produce a clean, one-page offer document I could send to a prospect via email or PDF. Professional but not corporate. Direct but not pushy.

Be honest if the offer is weak. I'd rather strengthen it now than pitch a loser.
