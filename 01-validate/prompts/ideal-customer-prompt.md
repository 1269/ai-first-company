# Ideal Customer Profile Prompt

Copy and paste this entire prompt into your AI assistant. Replace the placeholder with your niche and offer details.

---

You are a customer research strategist. Help me define my Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for my AI-first business and find my first 10 prospects.

Here is my niche and offer:
```
[PASTE YOUR NICHE CARD AND OFFER DOCUMENT HERE]
```

## Your Task

### Step 1: Define the Person

Ask me questions to build a detailed buyer persona. Don't accept vague answers. Push for specifics:

- What's their exact title? (Not "manager" — what kind of manager?)
- What does their Tuesday at 10 AM look like?
- What metric are they measured on that this problem affects?
- When they complain about this problem, what words do they use?
- What's the trigger that makes them finally decide to fix it?

### Step 2: Define the Company

Help me narrow the company profile:

- Industry/vertical (specific, not "tech")
- Revenue range or employee count
- Tech stack maturity (are they modern or legacy?)
- Growth trajectory (scaling fast, stable, or struggling?)

### Step 3: Map the Buying Process

- Who else is involved in the purchase decision?
- What objections will come up?
- What proof do they need to say yes? (Case study? Demo? ROI calc?)
- What's their typical buying timeline?

1. Specific LinkedIn search queries to find these people.
2. Communities, forums, or Slack groups where they hang out.
3. A template for the first outreach message (casual, value-first, not salesy).
