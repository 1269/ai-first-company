# Ideal Customer Profile

**Phase 1: Validate → Step 2**

Your offer is only as good as the person you're putting it in front of. This step defines exactly who that person is — not a demographic, but a real human with a real problem and a real budget.

## The ICP Framework

### The Person (Not the Company)

Companies don't buy. People do. Define the person:

- **Title:** What's their job title? (VP of Sales, Head of Operations, Agency Owner)
- **Daily pain:** What frustrates them every single day?
- **Failed solutions:** What have they already tried that didn't work?
- **Budget authority:** Can they spend $5K-$50K without committee approval?
- **Trigger event:** What moment makes them say "I need to fix this NOW"?

### The Company

- **Size:** Revenue range or employee count.
- **Industry:** Specific vertical, not "all businesses."
- **Tech maturity:** Do they already use modern tools, or are they on spreadsheets?
- **Growth stage:** Scaling (need efficiency) or struggling (need transformation)?

### The Buying Context

- **Where do they look for solutions?** (LinkedIn, Google, conferences, referrals, communities)
- **Who influences the decision?** (IT team? CFO? Their assistant?)
- **What objections will they raise?** (Price, security, "we tried AI before," timeline)
- **What makes them say yes?** (ROI proof, case study, guarantee, peer recommendation)

## The One-Sentence Test

Can you complete this sentence?

> "My ideal customer is a [title] at a [type of company] who is struggling with [specific problem] and currently wastes [time/money] on [current bad solution]."

If you can't fill that in with specifics, you don't know your customer yet.

## Finding Your First 10

Once your ICP is defined, find 10 real people who match it:

1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — Search by title, company size, industry.
2. **Industry communities** — Slack groups, Discord servers, Reddit, forums.
3. **Conference attendee lists** — People who attend events in your niche.
4. **Competitor customers** — Who's using the inferior solution today?
5. **Your existing network** — Who do you already know that fits?

You don't need thousands. You need 10 conversations to validate your offer.

## Output

After this step, you should have:

1. **ICP document** — A detailed profile of your ideal buyer.
2. **List of 10 real prospects** — Names, companies, titles, LinkedIn URLs.
3. **Outreach plan** — How you'll start conversations with each one.
