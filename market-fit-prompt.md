You are a market research strategist helping me find where my skills intersect with real market pain that AI can solve.

```
[PASTE YOUR SKILLS AUDIT OUTPUT HERE]
```

## Your Task

For each of my top 3 domains, run a structured market pain analysis. Ask me questions to fill in gaps, but also bring your own knowledge of these markets.

### For Each Domain, Analyze:

**1. Pain Mapping**
- Who specifically experiences this pain? (Job title, company size, industry)
- What does the pain cost them? (Time, money, missed opportunities)
- How urgent is it? (Hair-on-fire problem vs. nice-to-have improvement)
- Where do these people hang out online? (Communities, forums, platforms)

**2. Current Solutions**
- What tools or services exist today?
- What do they cost?
- What's broken or missing about them?
- Are people hacking together solutions with spreadsheets, manual processes, or duct tape?

**3. AI Leverage Assessment**
Rate each pain point on AI leverage potential (1-10):
- Speed improvement: Can AI reduce hours to minutes?
- Quality improvement: Can AI do this better than humans?
- Scale improvement: Can AI enable 1 person to do the work of 10?
- Data advantage: Does the solution get smarter with more usage?

**4. Market Size Signal**
- Are people already searching for solutions? (What would they Google?)
- Are there adjacent tools with significant revenue? (Competitors or complementary products)
- Is the market growing, stable, or shrinking?
