# Offer Design

**Phase 1: Validate → Step 1**

You have a niche. Now you need an offer — something specific you sell for a specific price to a specific person.

This is where most AI founders get stuck. They build a tool and hope people buy it. Instead, design the offer first, sell it, then build the tool.

## The Value Equation

Every offer lives or dies by this equation (credit: Alex Hormozi):

```
            Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
            Time to Result × Effort Required
```

To maximize value:
- **Increase** the dream outcome (make it bigger, more specific).
- **Increase** the perceived likelihood (proof, guarantees, credibility).
- **Decrease** the time to result (faster = more valuable).
- **Decrease** the effort required (done-for-you > done-with-you > do-it-yourself).

AI is uniquely powerful here because it can dramatically reduce **time to result** and **effort required** simultaneously.

## Offer Tiers

Most AI-first companies should start with services and ladder toward products:

### Tier 1: Done-For-You (DFY) — Start Here
- **What:** You build the AI solution for the client.
- **Price:** $5,000 - $50,000+ per project (value-priced).
- **Margin:** High, but time-intensive.
- **Purpose:** Generate revenue, learn what clients actually need, build case studies.

### Tier 2: Done-With-You (DWY) — Scale With
- **What:** You guide the client through implementation with templates and support.
- **Price:** $1,000 - $10,000 per engagement.
- **Margin:** Higher than DFY, more clients simultaneously.
- **Purpose:** Leverage your knowledge without doing all the work.

### Tier 3: Do-It-Yourself (DIY) — Scale To
- **What:** Productized tool, course, or template the client uses independently.
- **Price:** $50 - $500/month or one-time.
- **Margin:** Highest at scale, requires significant upfront investment.
- **Purpose:** Passive/semi-passive revenue at scale.

**The ladder:** Start with Tier 1 to learn. Use what you learn to build Tier 2 and 3.

## Designing Your First Offer

Answer these questions to define your v1 offer:

1. **Who is the buyer?** (Name the role. Not "businesses" — a specific person with budget authority.)
2. **What is the dream outcome?** (Not "AI automation" — what changes in their life/business?)
3. **What does the deliverable look like?** (Be concrete. An agent? A workflow? A dashboard?)
4. **How long does it take?** (Scope it. 1 week? 4 weeks? Ongoing?)
5. **What's the price?** (Based on value, not hours. What's the outcome worth to them?)
6. **What's the guarantee?** (How do you reduce their risk?)

After this step, you should have:

3. **Scope boundaries** — What's included, what's not, what costs extra.
