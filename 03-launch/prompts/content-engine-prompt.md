# Content Engine Prompt

Copy and paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

---

You are a content strategist helping me build a sustainable content engine for my AI-first business. I'm a solo operator — the system needs to be efficient, not exhausting.

Here is my niche:
```
[PASTE YOUR NICHE CARD HERE]
```

My content experience: [DESCRIBE — e.g., "Never published anything" or "I've been posting on LinkedIn for 6 months"]

My available time for content: [HOURS PER WEEK — be realistic]

## Your Task

### Step 1: Define Content Pillars

Help me identify 3-4 content pillars tied to my niche. Each pillar should:
- Demonstrate my expertise.
- Address a pain my ideal customer has.
- Be something I can create content about for 12+ weeks without running dry.

### Step 2: Choose Primary Platform

Ask me about my strengths and preferences to recommend ONE primary platform:
- Am I comfortable on camera? → YouTube
- Do I write well? → Newsletter / Blog / LinkedIn
- Am I good at short-form? → X/Twitter or LinkedIn posts

One platform. Master it. Expand later.

### Step 3: Create the 12-Week Content Calendar

Generate a 12-week content plan:

```
## 12-Week Content Calendar

### Week 1
- **Long-form:** [Topic] — Pillar: [X]
- **Repurposed:** [3-5 short-form pieces derived from the long-form]

### Week 2
[Same structure]

...through Week 12
```

Alternate between pillars. Build in variety. Make sure at least 2 of the 12 weeks include offer-related content (case study, behind-the-scenes of delivering results).

### Step 4: Repurposing System

Define the exact repurposing workflow:
1. Publish long-form on [day].
2. Extract [X] short-form pieces.
3. Schedule across [platforms] using [tool].
4. Total time per week: [X hours].

### Step 5: Content Prompts

For each of the first 4 weeks, generate:
- A working title.
- A 3-bullet outline.
- A hook (first sentence or first 5 seconds).

Be specific to my niche — generic content advice is useless.
