# Lead Generation Prompt

Copy and paste this prompt into your AI assistant.

---

You are a lead generation strategist for solo AI-first operators. Help me build a lead gen system that generates qualified conversations without requiring a sales team.

Here is my niche and offer:
```
[PASTE YOUR NICHE CARD AND OFFER DOCUMENT HERE]
```

Here is my ICP:
```
[PASTE YOUR IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE HERE]
```

## Your Task

### Step 1: Lead Magnet Design

Help me create a lead magnet that my ICP actually wants. Ask me:
- What's the most common question my ICP asks?
- What tool, template, or checklist would save them hours?
- What small win can I deliver for free that makes them want the full solution?

Design a lead magnet:
- **Name:** [Compelling title]
- **Format:** [PDF checklist, interactive tool, video walkthrough, template]
- **What it delivers:** [Specific quick win]
- **CTA:** [What happens after they download — email sequence → discovery call]

### Step 2: Outbound System

Design a weekly outbound cadence:
- Where to find prospects (specific platforms, communities, search queries).
- How to warm them up before reaching out.
- Template for value-first outreach messages (3 variants for A/B testing).
- Follow-up sequence (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14).

### Step 3: Inbound System

Connect content to leads:
- How to include CTAs in my content without being salesy.
- Email nurture sequence (3-5 emails that build trust and present the offer).
- Discovery call booking flow.

### Step 4: Pipeline Tracker

Create a simple pipeline tracking template:

```
| Prospect | Company | Status | Last Touch | Next Action | Notes |
|----------|---------|--------|------------|-------------|-------|
```

With status stages:
1. Identified → 2. Contacted → 3. Responded → 4. Call Booked → 5. Proposal Sent → 6. Closed Won/Lost

### Step 5: Weekly Metrics

Define the 5 numbers I should track weekly:
1. [Metric] — Target: [X]
2. [Metric] — Target: [X]
3. [Metric] — Target: [X]
4. [Metric] — Target: [X]
5. [Metric] — Target: [X]

Keep the system simple enough that I can run it in 2 hours/week.
