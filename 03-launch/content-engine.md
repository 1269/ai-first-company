# Content Engine

**Phase 3: Launch → Step 1**

Content is how an AI-first solo operator builds trust at scale. You can't take 1,000 sales calls a week, but you can publish content that does the selling for you while you sleep.

This isn't about becoming a "content creator." It's about building a machine that consistently puts your expertise in front of the right people.

## The Content Flywheel

```
Create Long-Form (weekly)
        ↓
Repurpose to Short-Form (3-5x/week)
        ↓
Drive to Lead Magnet / Offer
        ↓
Leads → Conversations → Revenue
        ↓
Revenue funds more content
        ↓
(repeat)
```

## The One-Video-Per-Week System

Start with one long-form piece per week. For most AI-first founders, this is a YouTube video, but it could be a blog post, podcast episode, or newsletter. Pick one format and commit for 12 weeks.

### Weekly Content Flow

| Day | Action | Time |
|-----|--------|------|
| **Thursday** | Script/outline the video. | Deep Creative block |
| **Saturday** | Record the video. | Morning block |
| **Monday** | Edit and add graphics/B-roll. | Deep Creative block |
| **Monday** | Publish video + write social posts. | Production block |
| **Tues-Fri** | Repurposed content goes out on social. | Automated |

### Content Pillars

Every piece of content should fit one of 3-4 pillars tied to your niche:

1. **How-To / Tutorial** — "Here's how I built [thing] using AI." Shows expertise.
2. **Behind-the-Scenes** — "Here's what happened this week building my AI company." Builds trust.
3. **Opinion / Hot Take** — "Here's why [common approach] is wrong." Attracts attention.
4. **Case Study / Results** — "Here's what happened when I did [thing] for a client." Proves results.

Each pillar maps to a stage of buyer awareness:
- How-To → Attracts people who know they have a problem.
- Behind-the-Scenes → Builds relationship with people following your journey.
- Opinion → Attracts new audience who don't know you yet.
- Case Study → Converts people who are considering buying.

## Repurposing Framework

One long-form piece becomes 5-10 pieces of short-form content:

From a single YouTube video:
- 1 LinkedIn article or post (key insight from the video).
- 2-3 tweet/X threads (individual tips or takeaways).
- 1-2 short-form video clips (30-60 second highlights).
- 1 newsletter segment (summary + link to full video).
- 1 blog post (expanded version with details the video didn't cover).

**The content repurposing automation** (see `../02-build/automation-playbooks.md`) handles most of this.

## The 12-Week Content Sprint

Week 1-4: **Find your voice.** Publish weekly. Don't worry about quality. Get reps in.
Week 5-8: **Refine and engage.** Notice what resonates. Double down on topics that get traction. Start conversations with people who engage.
Week 9-12: **Convert.** Reference your offer in content. Include clear CTAs. Start driving to discovery calls.
