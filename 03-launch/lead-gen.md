# Lead Generation

**Phase 3: Launch → Step 2**

Content builds awareness. Lead gen turns awareness into conversations. Conversations turn into revenue.

For a solo AI-first operator, the goal isn't "more leads." It's **more conversations with the right people.** You only need 2-4 clients at a time if you're doing Done-For-You work at $5K-$50K per project.

## The Lead Gen Stack

### Inbound (Content-Driven)

Your content engine (see `content-engine.md`) drives inbound leads:

1. **Content → Lead Magnet** — Offer something valuable (template, audit, tool) in exchange for contact info.
2. **Lead Magnet → Email Nurture** — Short 3-5 email sequence that builds trust and presents your offer.
3. **Email → Discovery Call** — Clear CTA to book a call when they're ready.

### Outbound (Proactive)

While inbound builds, outbound fills the pipeline now:

1. **ICP Targeting** — Use your Ideal Customer Profile to find specific people.
2. **Warm Outreach** — Engage with their content first. Comment, share, add value.
3. **Value-First DM/Email** — Lead with insight, not a pitch. Share something useful for their specific situation.
4. **Conversation → Discovery Call** — If they're interested, offer to dig deeper on a call.

### AI-Powered Lead Gen

This is where being AI-first gives you leverage:

- **AI Research Agent** — Monitors industry news, job postings, and company announcements to identify trigger events (new funding, new hire, expansion) that signal buying intent.
- **AI Personalization** — Generates custom outreach messages based on the prospect's recent content, company context, and specific pain points.
- **AI Lead Scoring** — Ranks leads by likelihood to convert based on engagement signals.

See `agents/templates/` for starter agent configurations.

## The Weekly Lead Gen Cadence

| Day | Action | Volume |
|-----|--------|--------|
| **Monday** | Review lead pipeline. Prioritize follow-ups. | 15 min |
| **Tuesday** | Outbound: research 10 prospects. | 30 min |
| **Wednesday** | Outbound: send 5-10 personalized messages. | 30 min |
| **Thursday** | Inbound: review lead magnet signups. Nurture. | 15 min |
| **Friday** | Follow up on all open conversations. | 20 min |

Total: ~2 hours/week. That's it. More than this as a solo operator and you're either targeting too broadly or your messaging isn't sharp.

## Metrics That Matter

Track weekly:

- **Conversations started** — New meaningful exchanges with potential buyers.
- **Discovery calls booked** — People who want to talk about your offer.
- **Proposals sent** — Active opportunities in the pipeline.
- **Close rate** — Proposals that convert to paying clients.
- **Revenue** — The only metric that pays rent.
